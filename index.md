## Terms of Service {#terms}

Last updated: 17 November 2026

### What the app does

PeakClips Uploader is a private tool used by the operator of the PeakClips channel. It prepares short highlight clips from live streams and, only after the account owner has reviewed and approved each clip, uploads it to the account owner's own TikTok account through the official TikTok Content Posting API.

### Who may use it

The app is intended solely for the operator of PeakClips and the TikTok accounts the operator owns. It is not offered to the general public. By connecting a TikTok account you confirm that you own that account and are authorised to publish content on it.

### Content and rights

The account owner is responsible for every clip published through the app and for holding the necessary rights or permissions, for example through a creator's clipping programme. Clips are only published after explicit approval. The app must not be used to publish content that violates TikTok's Terms of Service, Community Guidelines or the rights of third parties.

## Privacy Policy {#privacy}

Last updated: 17 November 2026

### Controller

The controller is the operator of the PeakClips channel, based in Germany. See the contact section below.

### Data we receive from TikTok

After you log in with TikTok, the app receives basic account information (open ID, display name, avatar), access and refresh tokens that allow it to upload videos to your account, and status information about uploads, for example whether a post was published.

### Purpose

This data is used exclusively to upload clips that you have approved to your own TikTok account and to show you whether the upload succeeded. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

### Storage

Tokens and upload logs are stored locally on the operator's own computer and are not transferred to any other server, except to TikTok itself when a video is uploaded.

### Sharing

No personal data is sold, rented or shared with third parties. Data is only sent to TikTok as required to perform the upload.

### Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only as long as the app is connected. When you revoke access in your TikTok settings, the tokens become invalid and are deleted locally. Upload logs are deleted after 90 days at the latest.

### Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

### Contact

For any questions about these terms or your data, contact the PeakClips operator by direct message to the PeakClips account on TikTok.

PeakClips is not affiliated with TikTok.
